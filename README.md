## About

Haqdar is an AI-powered legal assistant chatbot built specifically for Pakistani women. It provides legal information about women's rights in simple Urdu, making justice accessible to everyone — regardless of education level or technical literacy.

Millions of women in Pakistan face legal challenges daily — divorce, inheritance disputes, domestic violence, workplace harassment — but lack access to affordable legal guidance. Haqdar bridges this gap by putting legal knowledge directly in their hands, in their own language.

---

## Problem Statement

- 77% of Pakistani women are unaware of their basic legal rights.
- Legal consultation is expensive and inaccessible for most women.
- Online legal resources are mostly in English, creating a significant language barrier.
- Women in rural areas have no access to lawyers or legal aid services.

---

## Solution

A simple, mobile-friendly web application where women can ask legal questions in Urdu and receive instant, easy-to-understand answers powered by Google Gemini AI. The app provides quick access to information on six common legal topics and displays emergency helpline numbers at all times.

---

## Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Urdu Interface | Full RTL Urdu UI — no English required |
| AI Chatbot | Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash |
| Quick Topics | Six one-tap buttons for common legal questions |
| Mobile Friendly | Optimized for low-end Android phones |
| Private & Safe | No user data stored — completely anonymous |
| Emergency Helplines | Women Helpline 1099 always visible |

---

## Legal Topics Covered

- **Divorce (Talaq)** — Rights under Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961
- **Inheritance (Wirasat)** — Daughters' share under Pakistani succession law
- **Domestic Violence** — Punjab Protection of Women Against Violence Act 2016
- **Khula** — Step-by-step process for women-initiated divorce
- **Child Custody (Hizanat)** — Custody laws in Pakistan
- **Workplace Harassment** — Protection Against Harassment of Women at Workplace Act 2010
